MoyCheese Crew Member Application

Practice filling out a job application for a restaurant, the kind many people fill out for their first job. Choose a clean version to build speed and comfort, or a version with real accessibility problems built in on purpose, the same kind found on real job application sites.

What to expect

You will apply as a made up person, never as yourself. When you begin, you get a scenario with that person's details, and you can open it again at any time.

This form has ten sections: the position, about you, school, availability, experience, the Utah food handler permit, two making change questions, why you want the job, two references, and a final agreement you sign by typing a name.

Choose Accessible below to practice on a clean, correctly built copy of the form. Choose Not Accessible to practice on a copy with real problems in it on purpose. In that mode you still submit from the Not Accessible tab, and you can switch to the Accessible tab any time to check how something should have been built.

Some questions have a short "Why they ask" note that explains what the manager is looking for.

Nothing on this page is a dead end, and nothing you type is saved or sent anywhere on its own. When you submit, you get an interview code for the next activity, and you can choose to copy a practice summary or email it to your teacher.

Choose your screen reader before you begin.
