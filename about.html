About ScreenPath

Built for every student
who ever felt left out.

A free accessible learning platform for screen reader users. No cost. No login. No barriers.

I built this for my students. And for every student who has ever felt like the technology was the obstacle.

There was a moment in my life when I had to learn to navigate the world in a completely different way than I had planned. Gradually. Quietly. And mostly on my own.

Nobody had been through it before me, at least not anyone I knew. There was no roadmap. I found programs that taught me the basics but not enough to get me where I needed to go. Most of what I know today I taught myself. Podcasts at night. YouTube on weekends. User guides until things finally clicked. Hundreds of hours learning by doing until one day navigating the web independently felt not just possible but natural.

That feeling changed everything for me.

I did not want my students to have to find their way the same way I did. Alone. By trial and error. Without something designed specifically for them. That is why ScreenPath exists.

What ScreenPath is for

ScreenPath is not here to teach screen reader navigation from scratch. Your teacher does that. ScreenPath is here for what comes after.

After you have the foundation. After you know the skills but they do not feel like yours yet. After you want to get faster, more confident, more natural.

That is the space ScreenPath fills. Every activity is designed so the technology disappears into the experience. You stop thinking about the screen reader and start thinking about what you are actually doing with it.

How your results are saved

When you finish an activity, ScreenPath saves a few basic things, like how long it took, how many attempts you made, and whether you finished. That is all.

No names. No personal information. Nothing is sent anywhere automatically. Everything stays on the device you are using, and you can see it anytime on the My Progress page.

At the end of most activities you will see a summary with three choices:

  • Email: opens your email app with your results filled in, ready to send to your teacher.
  • Copy: copies your results so you can paste them anywhere.
  • Close: moves on with no pressure. Your progress is still saved on this device.

Teachers can find more about using ScreenPath in class on the For Teachers page.
