About ScreenPath

Built for every student
who ever felt left out.

A free accessible learning platform for screen reader users. No cost. No login. No barriers.

I built this for my students. And for every student who has ever felt like the technology was the obstacle.

There was a moment in my life when I had to learn to navigate the world in a completely different way than I had planned. Gradually. Quietly. And mostly on my own.

Nobody had been through it before me, at least not anyone I knew. There was no roadmap. I found programs that taught me the basics but not enough to get me where I needed to go. Most of what I know today I taught myself. Podcasts at night. YouTube on weekends. User guides until things finally clicked. Hundreds of hours learning by doing until one day navigating the web independently felt not just possible but natural.

That feeling changed everything for me.

I did not want my students to have to find their way the same way I did. Alone. By trial and error. Without something designed specifically for them. That is why ScreenPath exists.

What ScreenPath is for

ScreenPath is not here to teach screen reader navigation from scratch. Your teacher does that. ScreenPath is here for what comes after.

After you have the foundation. After you know the skills but they do not feel like yours yet. After you want to get faster, more confident, more natural.

That is the space ScreenPath fills. Every activity is designed so the technology disappears into the experience. You stop thinking about the screen reader and start thinking about what you are actually doing with it.

How progress works

Results are stored in this browser on this device. Clearing browser data removes them. On a shared device, other people using this browser can see saved results. Copy or email results you want to keep before resetting progress.

Each completed room records the time used and the number of answer submissions. Results appear immediately in My Progress.

ScreenPath saves completed-room times, answer attempts, activity mode, and any optional reflection you add. You may choose to record which screen reader you use. No name or account is required. These results stay in this browser unless you choose to share them.

At the end of each activity you will see a summary. From there you have three options.

📧

Email Results

Opens your email app with everything already filled in. Add your teacher's email and press send.

📋

Copy Results

Copies everything to your clipboard. Paste it wherever you communicate with your teacher.

→

Continue

Closes the summary and takes you to the next step and optional reflection.

For teachers and AT specialists

Use it with your students. Share it with your colleagues. No permission required. No cost. Ever.

ScreenPath uses landmarks, headings, labeled fields, and keyboard controls to support screen-reader practice. Accessibility is ongoing work. Please report any barrier you encounter.

Intended for use with JAWS and NVDA on Windows and VoiceOver on Apple devices. The latest changes still need hands-on testing with those screen readers. The teacher guide includes a testing checklist.

One thing worth knowing. ScreenPath is a fluency tool, not a teaching tool. Students need the foundation before they arrive here. What this platform builds on top of that is speed, confidence, and independence.

If your student is still building that foundation, a structured teaching packet is currently in development. It will include lessons, practice exercises, and instructional videos designed to take a student from no screen reader experience to the point where they are ready to practice independently on ScreenPath. If you would like to be notified when it becomes available, reach out at screenpathedu@gmail.com and I will be in touch.
