The School Office

Where You Are

You stayed after school to study for tomorrow's math exam in Mr. Okafor's classroom. He unlocked it for you before he left and told you to be out by four thirty.

You got absorbed in your notes and lost track of time. When you finally looked up the hallway was dark and silent. You packed your bag and walked to the front door. Locked. You tried the side exit. Also locked. You reached for your phone. Dead.

The automatic locks engaged while you were still inside and nobody knows you are here. You are standing in the empty hallway of Westlake Middle School at 4:47 in the afternoon and you need to find a way out.

Next to the front doors, you find the emergency exit keypad. Its screen reads: "After hours exit. Enter the access code of tonight's on-call staff member." Below that, it shows a department and a job title, but no name and no code.

Every staff member's emergency access code is listed in the staff directory in the main office, just down the hall. The office door is unlocked, and the computer at the front desk is still open to the directory. Find the person who matches the keypad, get their access code, and get yourself out of here.

Screen Reader Skill Focus

Headings. Websites use heading levels like an outline. In this directory, each department is a level 3 heading, and each job title inside it is a level 4 heading. Moving by heading level lets you skip past everything in between instead of reading line by line.
