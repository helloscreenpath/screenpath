Rental Property Application

Practice filling out a realistic rental application with your screen reader. Choose a clean version to build speed and comfort, or a version with real accessibility problems built in on purpose, the same kind you may run into with real forms one day.

What to expect

This form has seven sections: your information, your current residence, your employment, your household, an emergency contact, move-in details and documents, and a final agreement you sign by typing your name.

Choose Accessible below to practice on a clean, correctly built copy of the form. Choose Not Accessible to practice on a copy with real problems in it on purpose: some fields missing labels, some groups not announced as groups, and at least one heading that only looks like a heading. In that mode you'll still submit from the Not Accessible tab, but the Accessible tab is one keystroke away any time you want to check how a field should have been built.

Nothing on this page is a dead end, and nothing you type is saved or sent anywhere on its own. At the end, you can choose to copy a practice summary or email it to your teacher.
