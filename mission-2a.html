Room 1: The Check-In Desk

Where You Are

It is Friday afternoon and you have big plans with your friends this weekend, maybe even a sleepover. Then you remember: your mom mentioned a doctor's appointment today. The last thing you want is to lose the whole afternoon sitting in a waiting room instead of hanging out with your friends. If you want any chance of making it to your friends on time, you need to move through this checkup fast.

You arrive at the front desk. The receptionist hands you a tablet and says she needs your registration finished now, while she still has an open slot for you. She only has a few minutes before she has to call the next patient in line, and if your form is not done in time, your spot goes to someone else and you wait even longer. Press Start below and your registration details will appear above the form, ready for you to match.
