Room 4: Out the Door

Dinner at Seven, Out the Door, Friday, 6:12 PM

Where You Are

It's 6:12. All three flags are cleared, and your total is on the screen. You bagged everything as fast as you could while you scanned. Then your phone buzzes. It's your brother.

Priya leans over. "Three quick checks and you're out the door. Check your bags, finish paying, and check your phone."

Three minutes until he pulls away.

Ready to Roll

Screen Reader Skill Focus

Each question is a group of radio buttons. Select exactly one answer per question.

This is different from Room 2, where checkboxes let you select all that apply. Read the table, order total, or messages before each question.

Sound effects:

Sound effects play during the room, including timer warnings and feedback on your answers. Turn them off here before starting if you prefer silence.

Dinner Is Saved.

You tap Pay, and the receipt prints. Priya moves the raw chicken into its own sealed bag, then slips cold liners into that bag and the bag with the milk, ice cream, and mozzarella. "Raw meat always rides alone," she says.

At 6:15, you push through the north doors just as your brother's car rolls up. The ice cream is still cold, all 12 items are packed safely, and nothing is missing.

At 7:00, your grandparents walk into a kitchen that smells like chicken parmesan. Grandpa takes one look at the table and grins.

You did this.

Friday, 7:00 PM, around the family table

I am so proud of you.

Tonight you sorted a messy list, read every label on a crowded shelf, caught three checkout mistakes, and still made it to the curb on time. You did not grab the first thing you found. You checked, and when something was wrong, you spoke up.

That is what independence looks like: reading carefully, checking your own work, and getting it done when people are counting on you. Your family sat down to dinner tonight because of you.

Moises
