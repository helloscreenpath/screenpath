Feedback

I want to hear from you.

Every message comes directly to me. I read each one personally and I take every word seriously.

Whether you found something that was not working, noticed something that felt off, have an idea for a new activity, or just want to say hello, I want to know. Every message helps me make ScreenPath better for everyone who uses it.

When you press the button below, your email app will open with everything already set up. My email address, the subject line, and a short example message will all be there. The example is just to help you think through what to share. Replace it with your own words, skip the parts that do not apply, or delete the whole thing and write whatever feels right.

There are no wrong answers. Just tell me what you noticed.

Here is what the email will look like

Hi Moises, I am a student reaching out about ScreenPath. I was on Room 2, The Library when the page stopped responding after I submitted my answer. I expected the next room to unlock but instead nothing happened and the page went quiet. This happened more than once. I also noticed the timer sound kept playing even after I submitted. Not sure if that is related.

This helps me find and fix problems faster, since the same page can behave differently in each screen reader.

These details will also be added to the end of your email. You can read or change them before you send it.
