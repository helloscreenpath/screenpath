Room 3: Growth and Vitals History

Where You Are

The hearing screening is behind you now, signed off and on file. The doctor flips open your chart, and you already know what is coming. Give this doctor one quiet moment and they will read through every year of your old charts out loud, height by height, appointment by appointment, until the whole visit stalls out. Once that starts, there is no cutting it short, you just have to sit through it.

Your growth and vitals history table is already on the screen. Your best move is to speak up before the doctor gets going: offer to help pull up the exact number they are after, so you land on the right visit together instead of drifting through years of old charts. It costs a few seconds to ask, but it saves you the whole ramble, and a few seconds is worth it if it gets you out the door faster. Press the button below to hear which comparison the doctor needs, then find it in the table before the five minute clock runs out.

Personal Vitals and Growth History Table

Screen Reader Skill Focus

Practice cross-row table navigation. Move through the table to locate specific visit records, read across a row for its full data, and compare weight, height, and BMI values across different visits.
