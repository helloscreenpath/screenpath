MoyCheese Online Order

Order lunch from MoyCheese, a burger and sandwich restaurant, using its online menu. Pick your food, choose pickup or delivery, add a tip, and check your total before you place the order.

What to expect

The menu has five sections, each with a heading and a table: Burgers, Sandwiches, Sides, Drinks, and Desserts. Check the box next to anything you want, then choose how many. Every time you add or change something, the page tells you what changed and your new total with tax.

After the menu, you choose pickup or delivery, pick a tip, and enter your name and phone number. Some fields have a short "Why they ask" note that explains what the restaurant is looking for.

Choose Accessible below to practice on a clean, correctly built copy of the order page. Choose Not Accessible to practice on a copy with real problems in it on purpose: some checkboxes with no names, a total that changes without telling you, and a section name that only looks like a heading. In that mode you can switch to the Accessible tab any time to check how something should have been built.

This is practice. No food is ordered and nothing you type is sent anywhere on its own.

Talk it over

Answer these with your teacher, or type your answers into the email before you send it.

  1. You have $15. Can you buy a Cheeseburger, French Fries, and a Fountain Soda once the 7 percent tax is added? How much would you have left?
  2. Why was your total more than the prices printed on the menu?
  3. Delivery costs $2.99 plus a tip. On a $12 order with a 15 percent tip, how much more do you pay for delivery than for picking it up yourself with no tip?
  4. The order page required your phone number but said your email was optional. Why might a restaurant need one but not the other?
