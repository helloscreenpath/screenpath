Start The School — Room 1For teachers

Three rooms. Practice at your own pace.

Ready to play: The School

The doors have locked after school. Find the codes, follow the clues, and use the computer lab to unlock the exit.

01

The School Office

Find a staff member and access code using headings and a directory table.

Skills: headings and tables

02

The Library

Follow a book trail across three shelves and uncover the terminal code.

Skills: links and tables

03

The Computer Lab

Complete an identity form using a fictional student profile.

Skills: form navigation and text entry

Your pace, your choice. Practice has no deadline. Challenge starts with 4, 5, and 4 minutes for the three rooms; you can choose 1–60 minutes per room.

Before you start: know how to move by heading, follow links, read tables, and enter text with your screen reader.

How it works

  1. Read the scene and task. Take time to understand what you need to find.
  2. Choose Practice or Challenge. Start when you are ready. Sound and scene narration are optional.
  3. Review what you accomplished. Your results stay in this browser. Share them with your teacher only if you choose.
