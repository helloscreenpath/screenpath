Practice headings, links, tables, and forms through story based challenges made for screen reader users.

For learners who already know the basics and want to become more independent.

Start The School: Room 1

  • Escape Rooms

    Step into a story where your screen reader is the key. Each escape room is set somewhere you know, with clues to find and puzzles to solve before time runs out.

    Skills practiced: moving through web pages, reading tables, and completing forms, with each room focused on one skill.

  • Arcade

    Quick games you can replay anytime to stay sharp between lessons.

    Skills practiced: listening, recall, and screen reader commands.

  • Skill Drills

    Focused practice on one skill at a time, built around tasks you will meet in everyday life.

    Skills practiced: typing, listening, and other everyday screen reader tasks.
