Escape Rooms

Story-based challenges told across several rooms. Each room practices one screen reader skill.

The School

The doors have locked after school. Find the codes, follow the clues, and use the computer lab to unlock the exit.

  1. 01

    The School Office

    Find a staff member and access code using headings and a directory table.

    Skills: headings and tables

  2. 02

    The Library

    Follow a book trail across three shelves and uncover the terminal code.

    Skills: links and tables

  3. 03

    The Computer Lab

    Complete an identity form using a fictional student profile.

    Skills: form navigation and text entry

Your pace, your choice. Practice has no deadline. Challenge starts with 4, 5, and 4 minutes for the three rooms; you can choose 1–60 minutes per room.

Before you start: know how to move by heading, follow links, read tables, and enter text with your screen reader.

The Doctor's Visit

Check in, get seen, and check out — a school health office visit told across four rooms.

  1. 01

    The Check-In Desk

    Update a patient's contact and history details in a check-in form before the appointment.

    Skills: edit fields and combo boxes

  2. 02

    The Exam Room

    Listen closely to the doctor's exam notes to catch the details that matter.

    Skills: auditory attention and listening

  3. 03

    Growth and Vitals History

    Read a table of vitals recorded over past visits to find the answer the doctor needs.

    Skills: table navigation

  4. 04

    The Front Desk

    Book a follow-up appointment using an interactive scheduling table.

    Skills: interactive tables and booking

Your pace, your choice. Practice has no deadline. Challenge gives you 5, 3, 5, and 5 minutes for the four rooms.

Before you start: know how to move by heading, follow links, read tables, and enter text with your screen reader.

The Grocery Store

Dinner at Seven. Your grandparents arrive at 7:00 and your brother can only pick you up at 6:15. Sort Mom's list, shop the aisles, beat the scanner, and get out the door.

  1. 01

    The Entrance

    Match every item on a jumbled grocery list to its store department, the same way a Canvas matching question works.

    Skills: matching questions and dropdowns

  2. 02

    The Aisles

    Read each shelf table and add exactly what is on the list, not the look alike sitting next to it.

    Skills: tables and checkboxes

  3. 03

    The Express Checkout

    Check three scanner flags against a price table, then decide whether to keep, swap, or ask for a price check.

    Skills: links and verification

  4. 04

    Out the Door

    Answer three quick checks about your bags, your total, and your brother's texts before he pulls away.

    Skills: radio buttons

Your pace, your choice. Practice has no deadline. Challenge gives you 6, 5, 4, and 3 minutes for the four rooms.

Before you start: know how to move by heading, follow links, read tables, and use dropdowns, checkboxes, and radio buttons with your screen reader.
