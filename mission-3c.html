Room 3: The Express Checkout

Dinner at Seven, The Express Checkout, Friday, 6:08 PM

Where You Are

It's 6:08. You roll up to the express lane with exactly 12 items and start scanning, bagging each item as you go. The screen beeps. Then again. Then a third time. Three items are flagged.

Priya walks over from her station. "Don't panic. The scanner flags stuff all the time. Sometimes it's right, sometimes it's wrong, and sometimes the store messed up. Check each one against your list and the prices, then decide."

Someone with a full basket is already waiting behind you. The line is getting longer.

Beat the Scanner

Screen Reader Skill Focus

Each flag has a link that jumps to that item's row in the price table, and each row has a link that jumps back to its flag.

Read the scanner message, then compare Mom's list, what scanned, and both prices. Decide with a button: Keep It, Swap It, or Ask for a Price Check.
