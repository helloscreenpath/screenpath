How your results are saved

When you finish an activity, ScreenPath saves a few basic things, like how long it took, how many attempts you made, and whether you finished. No names, no personal information, and nothing is sent anywhere automatically. Everything stays in this browser on this device.

At the end of most activities you can email your results to your teacher, copy them to paste somewhere else, or close the summary and move on. Your progress is saved here either way.
